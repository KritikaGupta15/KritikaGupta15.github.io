Publication

I am a co-author of a peer‑reviewed research paper published in IEEE titled "Regime-Aware Deep Learning Framework for Predictive Maintenance in Industrial Systems". This work focuses on Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction using NASA’s CMAPSS turbofan engine dataset.

We developed a regime‑aware deep learning approach that improves predictive accuracy across varying operational conditions by combining clustering-based regime identification with an LSTM + Attention model.
